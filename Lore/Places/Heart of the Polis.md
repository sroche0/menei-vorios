The walled city-center of [[Toudromi]], normally accessible only to a select few. Its recent, complete closure to anyone who isn't wealthy and doesn't own property inside the inner ring is a new development rather than tradition, and it has been deeply disruptive to daily life — even the residents trapped inside are confused by the decision and want to know why. Rumor from [[Hazelnuts]] says the lords of the city may be sick.
