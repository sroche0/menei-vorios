On the southern river, at a bit of a crossroads for commerce between regions.

Magic is not allowed in the city other then approved areas and is subject to arrest. The heart of the polis is only accessible to a select few and cant be freely entered. The closing of the heart of the polis is a new thing and is very disruptive to daily life. People who live there are confused by the decision and want to know why.
