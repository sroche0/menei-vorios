We take the left path because its the largest. [[Crimini]] drops a glowing stone on the ground a few feet into the tunnel to mark our path. A little ways down it opens up into a wider area, we cant even see the far side of it. along the right wall the tunnel continues on. The open space is full of foliage and we can see skeletons among it. Like the ones previously they appear gnawed on.

[[Raya]] bounces through the foliage barely touching a leaf and find a large pile of bones and the end of the chamber. She continues around the chamber and ranges slightly don the next tunnel. Everyone seems very interested in the bones in the middle of the foliage, they're all casting spells and whatnot. [[D'mia Vuurhand|D'mia]] walks up with [[Trevar]] on her back, i guess he didnt want to touch the mushroom? And his eyes were all glowy. weird guy, eh? Since everyone was so interested, i poked the pile and it collapsed. Before i could even say anything some kinda creature dropped from the ceiling and engulfed my head. It hurt like a sonofabitch, and i cant breathe.

### combat 
* I raged and ripped the thing off the first chance i got.
* we managed to kill them all in short order, without the element of surprise they werent much eh?

[[D'mia Vuurhand]] healed me up real good and i felt right as rain. Then [[D'mia Vuurhand]] and [[Crimini]] mended [[Steely Dan|steely dan]] who took quite a bit of damage. Then [[D'mia Vuurhand]] decided to huck a bone at another stalagmite on the ceiling. It was a helluva throw and actually knocked the thing down onto her. We looked around a bti but there was nothing in the bones that wasnt all digested. 

We kept going down the tunnel to an end. [[Trevar]] noticed that there was a crack that seemed weak and shot it with eldtritch blast and blew a hole in the wall. We moved through the hole and it opened up unto a large chamber we couldnt see the sides of. Weirdly there was a huge pool of water. At the very edge of what any of us can see there appears to be a tiny island in the center of the lake. [[D'mia Vuurhand]] casts a firebolt into the distance and we catch a glimpse of something on the island. I suggested that [[Steely Dan|steely dan]] just walks over, he doesnt need to breathe eh? [[D'mia Vuurhand]] wanders off while we're talking about it and found a raft.

[[Crimini]] and [[Tie Domi]] go across as they're the only two who would fit on at the same time. We push off and sort of row across to the island. In the center there appears to be a post with a lantern hanging from it over a strip of disturbed dirt. [[Tie Domi]] is convinced its a grave and that theres gonna be ghosts, he's terrified of ghosts because you can't punch them. [[Crimini]] tries to detect magic while i get everyone else across the water to the island. [[Trevar]] and [[D'mia Vuurhand]] recognize that the lantern is etched with the symbol of [[Athreos]] the boatman who guides lost souls. and radiates a faint necromantic energy. They also notice there used to be a coin that looks like it was taken recently. I put a coin down from my pouch, im not trying to risk any ghosts coming out. We go to leave and i tripped over a cylinder of metal. when i opened it there was a gentle repose scroll. We also notice the coin i'd left is now gone and everyone swears they didnt take it. We debate a bit about what to do and eventually decide to move on to the other side of the lake again. 

There is a small path on this side leading further undergound alongside a river. We continue down the path until we come to a waterfall. We're at the base of the waterfall and the river is very shallow here. [[Crimini]] notices a coinpurse at the bottom of the river under the waterfall. The pouch had 2 small healing potions in it. [[D'mia Vuurhand]] goes to climb the 10ft or so up the waterfall and i give her a boost. She comes out on the surface and sees the remains of a campsite in the distance but no people. [[Crimini]] and [[D'mia Vuurhand]] walk over to investigate the campsite. They discover the campsite appears to be the site of a battle. They also recognize  the ruined and discarded satchel of [[Ivandra]]. Inside is a note that says

```
Bring the ensorcelled egg to the [[cannoth estate]], the master will have further instructions there. 
  - K
```
