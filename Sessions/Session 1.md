We're asked to go down into the sinkhole  on the other side of the camp to explore and clear out monsters. We tried to lower [[Steely Dan]] down but one of the ropes broke and he was dropped partly down and he took some damage [[Crimini]] was able to repair it. 

[[Trevar]] casts something to scout ahead but doesnt find anything. We head out  down the tunnels vaguely southward with [[Raya]] out front. She keeps yelling at us with some spell to slow down for some reason. Eventually she comes across some old bones. [[Crimini]] investigates them and find they're 2 humanoid skeletons but not 2 full skeletons. [[Tie Domi]] recognized that the mold was "[[russet mold]]" which is poisonous and releases spores. 

[[Raya]] steps on some fungus as she was distracted by [[Tie Domi]] and [[D'mia Vuurhand|Dmia]]  talking about the spores. Theres a bug patch of it up ahead that she warns us about. [[D'mia Vuurhand|dmia]] Casts some fancy holy fire that burns the spores. As the fungus burns several Veggie pygmies emerge and attack us.

Most of the pygmies swarm [[Steely Dan]] and attack him. We were able to kill them all though [[Raya]] got downed and needed saving. [[D'mia Vuurhand|D'mia]] was able to save her. We took a short rest to catch our breath and then pushed forward. We can see a  three way split in the tunnel up ahead.
